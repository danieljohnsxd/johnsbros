Leadership

The people behind the work

DJ

Doby Johns

Founder & President

With a lifelong career in construction, starting from landscaping at age 8 to leading multi-million dollar projects, Mr. Johns evolved his brother’s company into a top civil contractor and then a comprehensive construction provider for both residential and commercial sectors. His extensive hands-on knowledge covers all design and construction phases. As president, he oversees operations and has built a skilled in-house team and a network of dependable subcontractors.

Co-Founder
