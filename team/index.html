Founder & President

Doby Johns

With a lifelong career in construction, starting from landscaping at age 8 to leading multi-million dollar projects, Mr. Johns evolved his brother’s company into a top civil contractor and then a comprehensive construction provider for both residential and commercial sectors. His hands-on knowledge covers every design and construction phase. As president, he oversees operations and has built a skilled in-house team and a network of dependable subcontractors.

Judah Johns

A go-to-market leader focused on business development and expansion into new markets.
