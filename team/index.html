Who we are

Meet the Team
behind JohnsBros

JohnsBros is a family owned business based in Texas with 50 years of expertise in design, construction, and development for residential and commercial sectors. We boast a skilled team of architects, engineers, and managers, committed to integrity, performance and punctuality.

Our Mission

At Johns Bros, our mission is to provide our clients with exceptional design, construction, and land development services that meet their unique needs and exceed their expectations. We are committed to delivering high-quality projects that are sustainable and innovative.

We believe in building lasting relationships with our clients, based on mutual trust and respect. Our team is dedicated to providing personalized services that address the specific needs of each client. We take a collaborative approach to each project, working closely with our clients to ensure that their vision is brought to life.

Core Values:

Dedication
We are dedicated to providing our clients with the highest level of service and quality. We take pride in our work and are committed to delivering projects that meet the highest standards.
Integrity
We believe in conducting our business with honesty, transparency, and integrity. We are committed to building lasting relationships with our clients based on mutual trust and respect.
Customer Focus
We take a customer-focused approach to each project, treating each client as an individual rather than just a number. We strive to exceed our clients’ expectations and are committed to their satisfaction.

Doby Johns

Founder & President

His extensive hands-on knowledge covers all design and construction phases. At JohnsBros, as president, he not only oversees operations but also has cultivated a skilled in-house team and network of dependable subcontractors.
